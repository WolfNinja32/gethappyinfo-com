# First-run / no last-good postcard fallback

Not model output. Checked-in craft for when there is no last-good new-shape
card. The pipeline substitutes today's `tasks.txt` line for `{{LINE}}` and
publishes the paragraph below. Each line gets its own paragraph (the deed must
appear in the text). Invents no seed and no real biography.

---

Someone meant to do it later and did it today instead. {{LINE}} It took almost
nothing — a minute, a look, a small reach across an ordinary day. Nobody asked
for a speech. The room, or the street, or the quiet between two people, shifted
half a degree warmer. That was enough. They went on with whatever came next,
lighter for having refused to skip it.
